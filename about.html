Meet Our Practitioner

Ten years of formal training, and a genuine commitment to treating the whole person.

Catherine

Catherine is our fully registered acupuncturist and Chinese herbal medicine practitioner. She studied Chinese medicine for ten years at Beijing University of Chinese Medicine — widely regarded as the leading institution for Chinese medicine training in the world.

Since establishing Adelaide Integrative Acupuncture in Valley View, she's built a practice around genuinely listening before treating — every patient's path back to balance looks a little different.

"Every patient's path back to balance is different — my role is to listen closely and treat the whole person, not just the symptom."
