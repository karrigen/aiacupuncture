How herbs are prescribed

Rather than a single herb for a single symptom, Chinese herbal medicine works with formulas — combinations of herbs balanced to work together, adjusted to your individual constitution and how your body responds over time.

Formulas are typically supplied as convenient granules that dissolve in hot water, though raw herb decoctions are also available for those who prefer the traditional method.

Is it safe alongside other medication?

Always let us know about any medications, supplements, or health conditions you have. Herbal medicine can generally be used alongside conventional treatment, but some combinations need care — this is exactly what your consultation is for, so nothing is left to guesswork.
