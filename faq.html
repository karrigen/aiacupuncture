Frequently Asked

Questions Patients Often Ask

Can't find what you're looking for? Just give us a call — we're happy to talk it through.

Does acupuncture hurt?

Most people feel little to nothing beyond a brief, light sensation as the needle is placed. Needles used are hair-thin and single-use. If you're needle-sensitive, cold laser therapy is a needle-free alternative worth asking about.

Do you accept HICAPS?

Yes — HICAPS is available, so you can claim your private health rebate on the spot rather than dealing with paperwork afterward.

Do I need a referral?

No referral is required. You're welcome to call or email directly to book a consultation.

How many sessions will I need?

It varies from person to person and depends on what you're being treated for. We'll give you a realistic estimate after your first consultation and adjust as we go, based on how you respond.

What should I wear to my appointment?

Loose, comfortable clothing is best, so points on the arms and legs are easy to access. We'll always work around what you're wearing if needed.

Can I combine herbal medicine with my regular medication?

Often, yes — but it's important to tell us about anything you're currently taking so we can prescribe safely. We'll always ask about this during your consultation.

Where are you located?

We're at 40 Knightsbridge Avenue, Valley View, SA 5093. See our Contact page for a map and directions.

Still have a question?

Reach out directly — there's no such thing as a silly question when it comes to your health.
