Acupuncture & Cold Laser Therapy

One of the most effective, natural approaches to health care, refined over thousands of years and now paired with modern cold laser technology for a gentler, pain-free option.

What is acupuncture?

Acupuncture works by stimulating specific points on the body, most often with fine, sterile, single-use needles. In Traditional Chinese Medicine theory, this encourages the body's own energy — Qi — to move freely along its natural pathways, restoring balance where it has been disrupted by stress, injury, or illness.

For those who prefer a needle-free option, cold laser (low-level laser) therapy stimulates the same acupuncture points using light instead, offering similar benefits with zero sensation at all — a good fit for children, needle-sensitive patients, or particularly delicate areas of the body.

Your first visit

Consultation & assessment. We start with a thorough conversation about your health history and what's brought you in, so treatment is tailored to you specifically.
Treatment. Needles or cold laser are applied to selected points while you rest comfortably, typically for 20–30 minutes.
Aftercare guidance. We'll talk through what to expect afterward and how many sessions are likely to help, based on what we find.
