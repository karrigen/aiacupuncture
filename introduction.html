About Us

Introduction

A brief look at the philosophy behind everything we do.

One connected system

Our human body is a whole system, where every part is connected to every other. Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) approaches health from this understanding — rather than isolating a single symptom, we look at how it fits into the bigger picture of your overall balance.

Qi, meridians, and balance

Central to TCM theory is Qi (pronounced "chee") — a vital energy that flows through pathways in the body called meridians. When Qi flows freely, the body tends toward balance. When it's blocked or depleted, discomfort and illness are thought to follow.

Acupuncture, cold laser, and herbal medicine each work, in their own way, to support this flow and restore equilibrium — the "balance and harmony" our clinic is named for.

Green, gentle, and time-tested

These are treatments with a long clinical history and, generally, minimal side effects — a genuinely natural option alongside, or instead of, more conventional approaches, depending on what's right for you.
