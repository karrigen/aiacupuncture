Evidence-Based Acupuncture

Classical theory, explained in terms that make sense today — so you understand not just what we do, but why.

Bridging tradition and research

Acupuncture's theoretical framework comes from Traditional Chinese Medicine, developed and refined over thousands of years of clinical observation. Alongside this, modern research has explored how acupuncture may influence the nervous system, circulation, and the body's natural pain-response mechanisms.

The strength of evidence varies by condition, and research in this space is still evolving. We think that's worth being upfront about — we'd rather have an honest conversation with you about what's realistic than overpromise.

What this means for you

During your consultation, we'll talk openly about what to expect, roughly how many sessions tend to help for your situation, and how we'll track whether treatment is actually working for you — so you're never just taking it on faith.

Our approach

We combine classical TCM diagnosis with a clear-eyed, practical view of what treatment can realistically achieve.

If acupuncture isn't the right fit for something you're dealing with, we'll tell you — and point you toward what might help instead.

Have questions about the evidence?

Ask us directly — we're happy to talk through what research does and doesn't currently say.
